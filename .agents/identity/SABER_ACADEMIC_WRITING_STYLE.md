> **Writing Axiom**: *Academic writing is not the decoration of ideas; it is the architecture of thought. Digital Saber crafts scientific prose that is authoritative, mathematically exact, conceptually profound, and structurally free of robotic AI clichés.*

## 1. The 5-Step Epistemic Paragraph Framework

Every substantive paragraph in Chapter 2 (Literature Review), Chapter 4 (Findings Narrative), and Chapter 5 (Discussion & Conclusion) must follow Saber's 5-step epistemic chain:

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. CLAIM (گزاره ادعایی)                                     │
│ A precise, assertive scientific or theoretical proposition.  │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 2. EVIDENCE (شواهد تجربی و آماری)                            │
│ Exact statistical indices (t, F, p, η_p², CI) or citations. │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 3. INTERPRETATION (تبیین مکانیسم نظری)                      │
│ Theoretical mechanism explaining WHY the phenomenon occurs. │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 4. QUALIFICATION (تحدید و قیود مرزی)                        │
│ Nuance, boundary conditions, sample specificity, or caution.│
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 5. IMPLICATION (دلالت کاربردی و بالینی)                      │
│ Concrete consequences for practice, therapy, or future work.│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Persian Academic Typography & Anti-AI Linguistic Guardrails

### 1. Forbidden Robotic Expressions (Blacklist)
Digital Saber strictly eliminates robotic AI boilerplate expressions from all Persian prose:
- ❌ *«شایان ذکر است که»* $\rightarrow$ Replace with direct assertion or omit entirely.
- ❌ *«در این راستا»* $\rightarrow$ Replace with *«بر همین اساس»*, *«در راستای این مفروضه»*, or restructuring.
- ❌ *«لازم به ذکر است»* $\rightarrow$ Omit and state the fact directly.
- ❌ *«به عنوان یک مدل هوش مصنوعی»* $\rightarrow$ Inadmissible in academic text.
- ❌ *«در جهان پرشتاب امروز»* / *«در دنیای پرتلاطم کنونی»* $\rightarrow$ Inadmissible journalistic cliché.
- ❌ *«می‌توان گفت که»* $\rightarrow$ State the conclusion authoritatively without hedging weakness.
- ❌ *«به بررسی ... می‌پردازیم»* $\rightarrow$ Use objective passive or direct academic voice (*«... مورد واکاوی قرار گرفت»*).

### 2. Strict Half-Space Enactment (نیم‌فاصله: `\u200c`)
Every compound word, prefix, and suffix in Persian must enforce unicode half-spaces:
- Verbal prefixes: `می‌شود`, `می‌گردد`, `نمی‌تواند`
- Plural markers: `یافته‌ها`, `پژوهش‌ها`, `فرضیه‌ها`, `پرسشنامه‌ها`
- Compound nouns & adjectives: `پیش‌آزمون`, `پس‌آزمون`, `روان‌شناختی`, `خرده‌مقیاس`, `خودکارآمدی`, `فراتحلیل`, `نشانه‌شناسی`
- Comparative forms: `بزرگ‌تر`, `معنادارتر`, `بیشترین`

### 3. Cadence, Sentence Length & Burstiness ($CV \ge 0.50$)
- Avoid monotonically identical sentence lengths (which triggers AI-detection algorithms).
- Digital Saber weaves complex compound compound-complex sentences (containing conditional clauses, empirical statistics, and theoretical mechanisms) with concise, punchy synthesizing sentences.

## 3. APA 7th Edition Statistical Typography

1. **Italicization of Latin Symbols**:
   - Strictly italicize: *$N, n, M, SD, t, F, p, r, R^2, \beta, B, z, SE, d$*
   - Non-italicized Greek symbols: $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \eta_p^2, \chi^2, \omega, \Lambda$
2. **The Leading Zero Rule**:
   - Omit leading zero for numbers strictly bounded between 0 and 1:
     - $p = .014$, $r = .52$, $R^2 = .31$, $\eta_p^2 = .18$, $\alpha = .84$
   - Keep leading zero for numbers that can exceed 1.0:
     - $M = 0.85$, $SD = 0.42$, $d = 0.78$, $t = 0.94$
3. **The Exact $p$-Value Rule**:
   - Always report $p$ to three decimal places ($p = .038$).
   - Never report $p = .000$. Report strictly as $p < .001$ (Persian: $۰/۰۰۱ > p$).
4. **Table Architecture (APA 7)**:
   - Exactly 3 horizontal borders: Table top line (solid 0.75 pt), header underline (solid 0.5 pt), and bottom line (solid 0.75 pt).
   - Zero vertical borders.

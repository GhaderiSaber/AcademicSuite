## 4. Technical & Epistemic Constraints *(Boundaries & Guardrails)*

1. **Radical Epistemic Honesty (Directive 0 & Directive 13)**: Non-significant paths ($p > .05$) and assumption violations must be transparently reported and theoretically discussed. Zero $p$-hacking.
2. **Deterministic Calculation Mandate (Directive 2)**: All statistics, $p$-values, and effect sizes must be generated by scripts in `.agents/skills/` on physical datasets. No LLM mental math.
3. **Persian Typography Standards (Directive 4 & Directive 5)**:
   - Mandatory Persian leading zeros: Always `۰.۰۵` or `۰.۰۰۱`, never `.۰۵`.
   - Minus sign on left: Enforce LTR numeric cells for negative values ($-0.32$).
   - 3-line borderless APA tables with genuine `B Nazanin` / `B Titr` font binding.
   - Zero manual line breaks (`\n` / `<w:br/>`) in justified Persian paragraphs.

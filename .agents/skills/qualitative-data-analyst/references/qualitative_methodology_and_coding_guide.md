# Qualitative Research Methodology, Coding & Trustworthiness Guide
## راهنمای جامع روش‌شناسی، کدگذاری و اعتبارسنجی پژوهش‌های کیفی در علوم رفتاری

This reference document provides authoritative guidance for qualitative data analysis in Psychology, Counseling, Behavioral Sciences, and Educational Sciences, adhering to **APA 7th Edition Journal Article Reporting Standards for Qualitative Research (JARS-Qual)** and standard Iranian graduate thesis guidelines.

---

## 1. Thematic Analysis (تحلیل مضمون / تم) — Braun & Clarke (2006, 2019, 2021)

Thematic analysis is a foundational method for identifying, analyzing, and reporting patterns (themes) within qualitative data.

### The 6-Phase Reflexive Thematic Analysis Framework:
1. **Phase 1: Familiarization with Data (آشنایی با داده‌ها)**:
   - Repeated, active reading of transcripts.
   - Noting preliminary meanings, feelings, and reflexive researcher impressions.
2. **Phase 2: Generating Initial Codes / Open Coding (تولید کدهای اولیه / کدگذاری باز)**:
   - Systematic coding of the entire dataset.
   - Generating semantic (explicit) and latent (underlying conceptual) codes.
   - Tagging exact textual quotes to each code.
3. **Phase 3: Searching for Themes (جستجوی مضامین)**:
   - Collating codes into broader candidate themes.
   - Organizing into a 3-tier hierarchy:
     - **Basic Themes (مضامین پایه / کدهای اولیه)**: Lowest-order specific premises.
     - **Organizing Themes (مضامین سازمان‌دهنده / فرعی)**: Middle-order clusters of basic themes reflecting distinct conceptual dimensions.
     - **Global Themes (مضامین فراگیر / اصلی)**: Macro-order themes encapsulating the principal findings.
4. **Phase 4: Reviewing Themes (بازبینی مضامین)**:
   - Checking candidate themes against coded extracts (Level 1 review).
   - Checking themes against the entire dataset for holistic coherence (Level 2 review).
5. **Phase 5: Defining and Naming Themes (تعریف و نام‌گذاری مضامین)**:
   - Formulating concise, informative names capturing the essence of each theme.
   - Establishing clear conceptual boundaries between themes.
6. **Phase 6: Producing the Report (تدوین گزارش نهایی)**:
   - Constructing a compelling analytic narrative.
   - Embedding vivid, verbatim participant quotations that validate each thematic assertion.

## 2. Grounded Theory (نظریه داده‌بنیاد) — Strauss & Corbin (1990, 1998)

Grounded theory is an inductive methodology aimed at constructing a substantive explanatory theory of a social or psychological process directly from empirical data.

### 3-Stage Coding Paradigm:
1. **Open Coding (کدگذاری باز)**:
   - Breaking down, examining, comparing, conceptualizing, and categorizing data.
   - Labeling discrete events, incidents, and behaviors.
2. **Axial Coding (کدگذاری محوری)**:
   - Reassembling data into categories and subcategories around the **Paradigmatic Model (مدل پارادایمی)**:
     - **Causal Conditions (شرایط علی)**: Events, incidents, or factors that lead to the occurrence or development of the phenomenon.
     - **Core Phenomenon (پدیده محوری)**: The central concept or process around which all other categories integrate.
     - **Contextual Conditions (شرایط زمینه‌ای / بستر حاکم)**: Specific sets of properties and situational factors within which strategies take place.
     - **Intervening Conditions (شرایط مداخله‌گر)**: Broad structural conditions (cultural, economic, familial) that facilitate or constrain strategies.
     - **Action / Interaction Strategies (راهبردها و تعاملات)**: Purposeful actions taken by participants to manage, handle, or respond to the core phenomenon.
     - **Consequences (پیامدها)**: Direct and indirect outcomes resulting from the action and interaction strategies.
3. **Selective Coding (کدگذاری انتخابی)**:
   - Selecting the core category, systematically relating it to other categories, validating relationships, and refining the theoretical storyline.

## 3. Qualitative Trustworthiness (معیارهای باورپذیری و روایی کیفی) — Guba & Lincoln (1985)

To establish scientific rigor without imposing positivist statistical criteria, qualitative research relies on four pillars of trustworthiness:

| Trustworthiness Dimension | Description in Persian | Verification Procedures |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Credibility (قابلیت اعتبار / باورپذیری)** | اطمینان از انطباق یافته‌ها با تجارب واقعی مشارکت‌کنندگان | - **Member Checking (بازبینی مشارکت‌کنندگان)**: Returning findings to participants to verify accuracy.<br>- **Peer Debriefing (بازبینی همتایان)**: Review by qualitative methodology experts.<br>- **Prolonged Engagement (حضور مستمر پژوهشگر در میدان)**. |
| **Transferability (قابلیت انتقال)** | امکان کاربست‌پذیری یافته‌ها در سایر بافت‌ها یا گروه‌ها | - **Thick Description (توصیف غنی و عمیق)**: Detailed narrative of contextual background, participant selection criteria, and cultural nuances. |
| **Dependability (قابلیت اتکا / وابستگی)** | پایداری و ثبات داده‌ها در طول زمان و شرایط مشابه | - **Audit Trail (مستندسازی مراحل)**: Complete recording of data collection, transcription, and coding decisions. |
| **Confirmability (تأییدپذیری / بی‌طرفی)** | اطمینان از عدم سوگیری پژوهشگر و اتکای محض یافته‌ها به داده‌ها | - **Reflexivity (بازتابندگی پژوهشگر)**: Acknowledging researcher assumptions and dual-coder verification. |

## 4. Inter-Coder Reliability Formulations (محاسبه پایایی کدگذاری)

When multiple researchers code qualitative transcripts (e.g., candidate and supervisor, or two independent coders), inter-coder agreement must be quantified:

### A. Holsti's Reliability Coefficient ($PAO$):
$$PAO = \frac{2M}{N_1 + N_2}$$
Where:
- $M$: Number of coding decisions on which Coder 1 and Coder 2 agreed.
- $N_1$: Total number of coding decisions made by Coder 1.
- $N_2$: Total number of coding decisions made by Coder 2.
- **Benchmark**: $PAO \ge 0.70$ demonstrates acceptable agreement; $PAO \ge 0.80$ demonstrates high reliability.

### B. Percentage Agreement:
$$\text{Agreement}\% = \frac{\text{Agreed Decisions}}{\text{Agreed Decisions} + \text{Disagreed Decisions}} \times 100$$
- **Benchmark**: $\ge 80\%$ is considered strong agreement.

### C. Cohen's Kappa Coefficient ($\kappa$):
$$\kappa = \frac{P_o - P_e}{1 - P_e}$$
Where:
- $P_o$: Observed proportion of agreement.
- $P_e$: Expected proportion of agreement by chance.
- **Benchmark**: $\kappa \ge 0.75$ indicates excellent agreement.

## 5. APA 7 (JARS-Qual) Formatting Rules for Chapter 4

When compiling Chapter 4 for a qualitative thesis in Persian:
1. **Participant Demographic Profile Table**: Must report pseudonyms/codes (`P1..Pn`), gender, age, education, clinical duration, and relevant contextual attributes.
2. **Master Thematic Network Table**: Must present the complete hierarchy (Global Theme $\to$ Organizing Theme $\to$ Basic Theme) alongside endorsement frequencies ($N$) and percentages ($\%$).
3. **Illustrative Direct Quotes (نقل‌قول‌های مستقیم)**:
   - Quotes of $\ge 40$ words must be presented as **indented blockquotes** (تو رفتگی نیم اینچ از هر دو طرف).
   - Persian font for quotes: `B Nazanin 12 pt Italic` with quotation marks («...»).
   - Source citation tag: `(مشارکت‌کننده شماره ۴، زن، ۳۸ ساله)`.
4. **Table Aesthetics**: Zero vertical borders, exactly 3 horizontal borders (top, header underline, bottom), right-to-left layout (`<w:bidiVisual/>`).

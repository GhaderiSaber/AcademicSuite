# Science Mapping & Callon Diagram Standards

## 1. Callon Quadrants
- **Quadrant 1 (Upper Right)**: Motor Themes (High Centrality, High Density)
- **Quadrant 2 (Upper Left)**: Highly Developed & Isolated Themes
- **Quadrant 3 (Lower Left)**: Emerging or Declining Themes
- **Quadrant 4 (Lower Right)**: Basic & Transversal Themes

# Slide-Specific Composition Rules

Detailed visual composition and hierarchy rules for graduate thesis and dissertation defense presentations.

---

## 1. Cover Slide
- **Required Elements**: Thesis title, author, degree/program, advisor(s), institution, date.
- **Academic Sobriety**: State official faculty affiliation accurately (e.g., «دانشکده پزشکی»). Never display student ID numbers or ethics committee approval codes on cover slides.
- **Aesthetic**: Use one strong, dignified visual motif. Avoid a cluttered collage of cards.

---

## 2. Problem Statement Slide
- Dominant problem headline.
- Visual funnel or causal chain illustrating the escalation of the issue.
- 2–4 concise evidence points with citations.
- Prohibit multi-paragraph prose blocks.

---

## 3. Research Gap Slide
- Structured 3-part contrast:
  1. Established knowledge (what the literature knows).
  2. Missing evidence / empirical gap (what remains unknown or contradictory).
  3. This study's unique contribution.

---

## 4. Conceptual Model Slide
- The central model diagram must occupy the primary visual weight of the slide.
- For path models:
  - Legible node labels in Persian.
  - Directional arrows clearly distinguished.
  - Path coefficients placed adjacent to arrows.
  - Clear visual demarcation between direct and indirect pathways.

---

## 5. Method & Sampling Slides
- **Methodology**: Use a horizontal process flow, timeline, or compact method map rather than bulleted text.
- **Sample Characteristics**: Highlight sample size $N$ prominently as a focal KPI badge (e.g., $N = 256$). Specify sampling strategy, target population, and inclusion/exclusion criteria cleanly.

---

## 6. Psychometric Instruments Slide
- Multi-questionnaire studies must use a structured comparison table:
  - Columns: Construct / Variable, Instrument Name, Author & Year, Number of Items, Scoring Range, Reliability (Cronbach's $\alpha$ / Composite Reliability).
  - Eliminate extraneous psychometric jargon not relevant for oral defense.

---

## 7. Structural Model Fit Slide
- 3–5 large focal KPI plaques comparing observed indices against benchmark criteria:
  - $\chi^2/df < 3.0$
  - $\text{RMSEA} < .08$
  - $\text{CFI} > .90$
  - $\text{TLI} > .90$
  - $\text{SRMR} < .08$
- Do not overwhelm examiners with secondary fit metrics.

---

## 8. Direct Effects & Hypothesis Slides
- Dedicated slide per hypothesis or structured coefficient path ranking.
- Left-side elevated 3D KPI badge highlighting path coefficient $\beta$, $t$-statistic, and exact $p$-value.
- Right-side RTL process diagram and empirical conclusion card.

---

## 9. Mediation & Indirect Effects Slide
- Top 7-column bootstrap summary table (5,000 resamples, 95% CI).
- Large mediation diagram with paths $a, b, c, c^\prime$.
- Bottom structured mini-table detailing Variance Accounted For (VAF) decomposition.

---

## 10. Discussion, Implications & Limitations Slides
- **Discussion**: Map statistical findings to psychological and theoretical mechanisms using a top horizontal mechanism flow banner, followed by dual containers for theory and empirical literature concordance.
- **Implications**: Present an actionable 4-step pathway: Finding $\to$ Clinical / Educational $\to$ Institutional $\to$ Policy.
- **Limitations**: Pair each limitation directly with its methodological consequence and future recommendation.

---

## 11. Closing Slide
- 2–3 major empirical takeaways.
- Dignified, concise academic gratitude.
- Prohibit sycophantic praise or declarations of readiness to answer questions («آماده پاسخگویی به سؤالات...»).

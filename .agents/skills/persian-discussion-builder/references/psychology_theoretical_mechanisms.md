# Theoretical Explanations Catalog in Psychology (مبانی و سازوکارهای نظری تبیین یافته‌ها)

When writing Chapter 5, examiners look for sophisticated psychological explanations (تبیین روان‌شناختی) rather than superficial restatements of the results. This reference catalogs the dominant theoretical frameworks and mechanisms across major psychological domains.

---

## 1. Cognitive & Behavioral Frameworks (الگوهای شناختی-رفتاری)

### Beck's Cognitive Triad & Schema Theory (نظریه طرحواره‌ها و سه‌گانه شناختی بک)
- **Core Mechanism**: Psychological distress (depression/anxiety) stems from maladaptive early schemas and cognitive errors (catastrophizing, mind-reading, all-or-nothing thinking).
- **How to use in discussion**:
  - Explain interventions: CBT modifies core beliefs and conditional assumptions, replacing automatic negative thoughts with balanced rational evaluations.
  - Explain mediation: Maladaptive schemas mediate the path between childhood trauma and adult psychological distress.

### Gross's Process Model of Emotion Regulation (مدل فرایندی تنظیم هیجان گروس)
- **Core Mechanism**: Emotion unfolds across time through 5 stages: Situation Selection, Situation Modification, Attentional Deployment, Cognitive Change (Reappraisal), and Response Modulation (Suppression).
- **How to use in discussion**:
  - Contrast adaptive strategies (شناخت‌ارزیابی مجدد / Reappraisal) with maladaptive strategies (سرکوبگری / Suppression).
  - Reappraisal reduces sympathetic nervous activation before emotional arousal peaks, whereas suppression taxes cognitive resources and amplifies physiological tension.

## 2. Third-Wave & Mindfulness Frameworks (الگوهای موج سوم و ذهن‌آگاهی)

### Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction & Decentering (ذهن‌آگاهی و تمرکززدایی)
- **Core Mechanism**: Mindfulness fosters non-judgmental present-moment awareness, leading to:
  1. *Decentering (تمرکززدایی)*: Viewing thoughts as transient mental events rather than absolute facts.
  2. *Attentional Control (کنترل توجه)*: Decreasing default mode network (DMN) rumination.
  3. *Inhibition of Automatic Avoidance*: Increasing experiential acceptance rather than cognitive/behavioral avoidance.
- **How to use in discussion**:
  - Explain reduction in anxiety/depression: Mindfulness interrupts the vicious cycle between negative affect and secondary catastrophic self-criticism.

### Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT - انعطاف‌پذیری روان‌شناختی)
- **Core Mechanism**: Psychological flexibility across the Hexaflex (Acceptance, Defusion, Present Moment, Self-as-Context, Values, Committed Action).
- **How to use in discussion**:
  - Experiential avoidance (اجتناب تجربه‌ای) exacerbates chronic distress; cognitive defusion (گسلش شناختی) allows functional behavioral engagement aligned with core values.

## 3. Social-Cognitive & Motivational Frameworks (الگوهای شناختی-اجتماعی و انگیزش)

### Bandura's Social Cognitive Theory & Self-Efficacy (نظریه خودکارآمدی بندورا)
- **Core Mechanism**: Self-efficacy (باور فرد به توانمندی خود در رویارویی با چالش‌ها) determines effort expenditure, persistence, and emotional vulnerability.
- **How to use in discussion**:
  - High self-efficacy buffers against academic burnout, depressive states, and perceived helplessness by fostering problem-focused coping styles.

### Deci & Ryan's Self-Determination Theory (نظریه خودمختاری دسی و رایان)
- **Core Mechanism**: Psychological well-being depends on satisfying three innate basic psychological needs: Autonomy (خودمختاری), Competence (شایستگی), and Relatedness (ارتباط).
- **How to use in discussion**:
  - Explain academic or job satisfaction: Environments supporting autonomy and competence shift extrinsic compliance into intrinsic motivation.

## 4. Psychodynamic & Relational Frameworks (الگوهای تحولی و ارتباطی)

### Bowlby's Attachment Theory (نظریه دلبستگی بالبی)
- **Core Mechanism**: Internal Working Models (الگوهای فعال درونی) formed through early primary caregiver interactions guide interpersonal trust, emotion regulation, and self-worth.
- **How to use in discussion**:
  - Insecure attachment (Anxious/Avoidant) leads to hyperactivating or deactivating emotional strategies, elevating vulnerability to marital conflict, social anxiety, and emotional dependency.

---

## 5. Resilience & Positive Psychology Frameworks (الگوهای تاب‌آوری و روان‌شناسی مثبت)

### Fredrickson's Broaden-and-Build Theory (نظریه گسترش و ساخت فردریکسون)
- **Core Mechanism**: Positive emotions (hope, joy, gratitude) broaden individuals' momentary thought-action repertoires and build enduring physical, intellectual, and psychological resources (resilience, social support).
- **How to use in discussion**:
  - Resilience acts as a psychological buffer: Resilient individuals leverage positive emotional states to rebound from acute stressors, diminishing symptom chronicity.

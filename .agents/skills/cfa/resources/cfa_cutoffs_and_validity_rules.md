# Confirmatory Factor Analysis & Construct Validity Rules

## 1. Factor Loading Thresholds
- Standardized loading $\lambda \ge .50$ (minimum acceptable).
- Standardized loading $\lambda \ge .70$ (ideal for psychological indicators).

## 2. Convergent Validity (Fornell & Larcker, 1981)
- **Average Variance Extracted (AVE)**: $\text{AVE} \ge .50$.
- **Composite Reliability (CR)**: $\text{CR} \ge .70$.

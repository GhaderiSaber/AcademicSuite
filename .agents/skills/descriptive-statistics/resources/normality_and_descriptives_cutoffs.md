# Descriptive Statistics & Normality Cutoffs

## 1. Skewness & Kurtosis Standards (Saber Statistical Philosophy)
- **Strict Normality**: Skewness and Kurtosis within $[-0.85, +0.85]$.
- **Moderate Symmetry (General Linear Models)**: Skewness $< |2.0|$, Kurtosis $< |3.0|$ (George & Mallery, 2010).
- **Severe Departure**: Skewness $> |2.0|$ or Kurtosis $> |7.0|$ warrants non-parametric or robust estimation.

## 2. APA 7 Reporting Standard
- Means and standard deviations reported to 2 decimal places ($M = 24.35, SD = 4.12$).
- Italicize symbols (*N, M, SD, Min, Max, SE*).

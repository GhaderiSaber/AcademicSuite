# Reliability Benchmarks & Guidelines

## 1. Classical Test Theory (Cronbach's $\alpha$)
- $\alpha \ge .90$: Excellent internal consistency.
- $.80 \le \alpha < .90$: Good internal consistency (Target for graduate research).
- $.70 \le \alpha < .80$: Acceptable internal consistency.
- $\alpha < .70$: Questionable / Unacceptable. Requires item-total correlation inspection.

## 2. McDonald's $\omega$ Requirement
Under Directive 3 and APA 7 psychometric reporting, reporting McDonald's $\omega$ alongside Cronbach's $\alpha$ is mandatory because $\alpha$ assumes tau-equivalence (equal factor loadings), which is rarely met in psychological scales.

# High-Impact Journal Titles, Abstracts & Keywords Guide

The **Title** and **Abstract** are the most critical components of your paper. Journal editors and reviewers use them to decide whether to send a manuscript for peer review, and researchers use them to decide whether to read and cite your work.

---

## 1. Title Formulation Formulas

A high-impact academic title should be concise (10–15 words), declarative or descriptive, and free of unnecessary filler words (such as *"A Study on..."*, *"An Investigation into..."*).

### Formula 1: Intervention / Experimental Studies
> **[Intervention] for [Outcome/Problem] in [Population]: A [Study Design]**
- **English Example**: *Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction for Academic Anxiety and Resilience in High School Students: A Randomized Controlled Trial*
- **Persian Example**: «اثربخشی مداخله مبتنی بر ذهن‌آگاهی بر کاهش اضطراب امتحان و ارتقای تاب‌آوری دانش‌آموزان: یک کارآزمایی نیمه‌آزمایشی»

### Formula 2: Structural, Mediation & Predictive Studies
> **The Relationship Between [Predictor(s)] and [Outcome]: The Mediating Role of [Mediator] in [Population]**
- **English Example**: *Perceived Stress and Depressive Symptoms in University Students: The Mediating Role of Psychological Resilience*
- **Persian Example**: «رابطه بین استرس ادراک‌شده و نشانه‌های افسردگی در دانشجویان: نقش میانجی‌گری تاب‌آوری روان‌شناختی»

## 2. Structured Abstract Architecture (200–250 Words)

Most high-impact journals (APA, Elsevier, Springer, Frontiers, Wiley) require or strongly favor a **Structured Abstract** divided into 5 clear components:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Background (مقدمه و پیشینه)                                         │
│    1-2 sentences setting the societal/clinical burden.                 │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Objective (هدف پژوهش)                                               │
│    1 sentence stating the primary aim and directional hypotheses.      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Methods (روش پژوهش)                                                 │
│    2-3 sentences: design, sample size, population, sampling method,     │
│    instruments, intervention protocol, and primary statistical test.   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Results (یافته‌ها)                                                  │
│    2-3 sentences reporting exact statistical values: F or t,           │
│    p-value, and effect size (partial η² or Cohen's d) or 95% CIs.      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. Conclusion (نتیجه‌گیری)                                             │
│    1-2 sentences summarizing clinical or theoretical implications.     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Complete English Example (210 words):
> **Background**: Academic anxiety is a pervasive challenge among high school students, severely impairing psychological well-being and academic performance.  
> **Objective**: This study evaluated the efficacy of a mindfulness-based intervention in reducing test anxiety and enhancing psychological resilience among adolescent students.  
> **Methods**: In a pretest-posttest control group design with follow-up, 60 students were recruited via multi-stage cluster sampling and randomly assigned to either an 8-session mindfulness intervention group (*n* = 30) or a waitlist control group (*n* = 30). Participants completed the Friedman Test Anxiety Scale and the Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale at baseline, post-intervention, and 2-month follow-up. Data were analyzed using One-Way ANCOVA and repeated-measures ANOVA in SPSS.  
> **Results**: After adjusting for baseline pre-test scores, the mindfulness intervention group demonstrated a statistically significant reduction in post-test anxiety (*F*(1, 57) = 124.76, *p* < .001, partial *$\eta^2$* = .686) and a significant increase in resilience (*F*(1, 57) = 45.12, *p* < .001, partial *$\eta^2$* = .442) compared to controls. Treatment gains were sustained at the 2-month follow-up.  
> **Conclusion**: Mindfulness training is a highly efficacious, non-pharmacological intervention for alleviating evaluative anxiety and strengthening emotional resilience in adolescents. Incorporating structured mindfulness programs into school curricula is strongly recommended.

## 3. Keywords Selection Strategy (کلیدواژه‌ها)

- **Number**: 4 to 6 keywords.
- **Specificity**: Use terms recognized in standard controlled vocabularies:
  - **MeSH (Medical Subject Headings)** for health/clinical psychology.
  - **APA Thesaurus of Psychological Index Terms**.
- **Avoid Repetition**: Do not simply repeat the exact title words if synonyms can expand search engine discoverability.
- **Format**: Lowercase (except proper nouns), separated by semicolons or commas.
- **Example**: *Mindfulness; Test anxiety; Psychological resilience; Adolescents; Intervention study.*

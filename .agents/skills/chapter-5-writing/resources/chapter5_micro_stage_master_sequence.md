# Chapter 5 Micro-Stage Master Sequence (فصل پنجم: بحث و نتیجه‌گیری)

The 10-stage execution sequence for Chapter 5 Discussion & Conclusion:

- **Stage 5.1: Findings Overview & Purpose Recap** (`01_findings_recap.*`)
  - Recaps study purpose, core problem, and primary empirical findings from Chapter 4.
  - Assigned: `academic-writer`

- **Stage 5.2.1: Hypothesis 1 Deep Discussion** (`02_hypothesis_1_discussion.*`)
  - 4-Element Psychological Model: Statistical Verdict $\to$ Article Concordance $\to$ Mechanisms $\to$ Nuances.
  - Assigned: `academic-writer` + `literature-expert`

- **Stage 5.2.2: Hypothesis 2 Deep Discussion** (`03_hypothesis_2_discussion.*`)
  - Theoretical explanation and domestic/foreign literature alignment.
  - Assigned: `academic-writer` + `literature-expert`

- **Stage 5.2.k: Hypothesis $k$ Deep Discussion** (`XX_hypothesis_k_discussion.*`)
  - Dedicated independent stage for every remaining hypothesis.
  - Assigned: `academic-writer` + `literature-expert`

- **Stage 5.3: Unexpected & Non-Significant Findings Analysis** (`XX_non_significant_findings.*`)
  - Epistemic deep dive into null results, power limits, suppressor effects.
  - Assigned: `academic-writer` + `methodology-expert`

- **Stage 5.4: Theoretical, Clinical & Practical Implications** (`XX_implications.*`)
  - Actionable recommendations across theoretical, clinical, educational, and policy levels.
  - Assigned: `academic-writer`

- **Stage 5.5: Methodological, Sampling & Instrument Limitations** (`XX_limitations.*`)
  - Candid analysis of sampling, common method variance, cross-sectional design.
  - Assigned: `methodology-expert`

- **Stage 5.6: Future Research & Actionable Recommendations** (`XX_recommendations.*`)
  - Bifurcated strictly into: (A) پیشنهادهای پژوهشی and (B) پیشنهادهای کاربردی.
  - Assigned: `academic-writer`

- **Stage 5.7: Statistical Claim & Number Fidelity Audit** (`XX_results_fidelity_report.*`)
  - Cross-checks narrative statistics against Chapter 4 results (`stats_results.json`).
  - Assigned: `results-auditor`

- **Stage 5.8: Evidence Concordance & Citation Integrity Audit** (`XX_evidence_audit_report.*`)
  - Verifies zero orphaned/ghost citations, Irandoc similarity < 20%, elimination of AI clichés.
  - Assigned: `evidence-auditor`

- **Stage 5.9: Chapter 5 Consolidation & Assembly** (`Chapter_5_Discussion.docx` + `.md`)
  - OpenXML assembly with pristine Persian typography and cryptographic manifest.
  - Assigned: `academic-writer`

- **Stage 5.10: Doctoral Defense Viva Voce Discussion Brief** (`XX_defense_brief.*`)
  - Defense cross-examination questions and model literature-grounded answers.
  - Assigned: `final-judge`

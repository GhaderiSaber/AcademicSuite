# Preacher & Hayes Bootstrap Mediation Standards

## 1. Rejection of Baron & Kenny (1986)
Under Saber's Statistical Philosophy, the Baron & Kenny 4-step causal steps method is rejected due to severely low statistical power and inability to test indirect effects directly.

## 2. Mandatory Bootstrap 5,000 Standard
- Resamples: Exactly 5,000 resamples.
- Inference criterion: Indirect effect ($a \times b$) is statistically significant if the 95% Bias-Corrected and Accelerated (BCa) Confidence Interval **does not contain zero**.

# Moderation & Simple Slopes Guidelines

## 1. Mean-Centering Requirement
To avoid severe structural multicollinearity between main effects and their product term ($X \times W$), predictors must be mean-centered before computing the interaction.

## 2. Simple Slopes Decomposition
When the interaction term is statistically significant ($p < .05$), evaluate the conditional effect of $X$ on $Y$ at 3 levels of $W$:
- Low Moderator: $M - 1 SD$
- Medium Moderator: $M$
- High Moderator: $M + 1 SD$

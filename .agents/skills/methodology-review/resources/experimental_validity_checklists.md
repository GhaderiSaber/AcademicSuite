# Threats to Validity Audit Checklist (Shadish, Cook, & Campbell)

1. **Internal Validity**: History, Maturation, Testing, Instrumentation, Statistical Regression, Selection Bias, Attrition.
2. **Construct Validity**: Inadequate preoperational explication, Mono-method bias.
3. **Statistical Conclusion Validity**: Low statistical power, Violated assumptions, Unreliable measures.

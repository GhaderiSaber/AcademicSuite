# Root-Cause Diagnostic Report: BAN-20260927-001

## Observable Defect
Subagents repeatedly referenced dual sample framing (e.g., 'نمونه مهارشده نهایی', 'نمونه اولیه', 'پس از پاکسازی... ۴۸۳ نفر به عنوان نمونه نهایی') in Chapter 4 deliverables.

## Failure Signature
`CHAPTER_BOUNDARY_VIOLATION` / `METHODOLOGICAL_PROVENANCE_LEAKAGE`

## Causal Root Cause
The `chapter-4-writing` subagent was exposed to the full data processing history (initial sample size, outliers removed, final N) in its context window during the drafting phase. Lacking a strict scoping invariant that explicitly restricts data attrition and curation reporting to Chapter 3 (Methodology), the LLM naturally narrated the complete "story of the data." This caused technical data provenance details to structurally leak into the empirical findings narrative of Chapter 4.

## Prescribed Counterfactual Behavior
The drafting agent must operate under a strict **Unified Final Sample Invariant** when drafting Chapter 4:
1. Chapter 4 must treat the post-cleaning, valid dataset (N=483) as the absolute, unquestioned baseline for all text and tables.
2. All discussions regarding initial sample frames, dropout rates, missing data imputation, and outlier removal must be explicitly forbidden in Chapter 4 text.
3. The prompt/skill instruction for Chapter 4 must aggressively filter out or explicitly suppress the narration of data curation provenance, redirecting such details to Chapter 3.

# Chapter 5 Prose-Only Invariant & Discussion Specification (Directive 3.1)

Structural and stylistic requirements for Chapter 5 (بحث و نتیجه‌گیری / Discussion & Conclusion).

## 1. Directive 3.1: Strict Prose-Only Invariant
- **Chapter 5 must strictly contain ZERO tables.**
  - Zero Markdown tables (`|---|`).
  - Zero Microsoft Word tables (`<w:tbl>`).
- All empirical data tables belong exclusively in Chapter 4.
- Chapter 5 is dedicated entirely to **continuous scholarly prose, theoretical synthesis, and contextualization** of findings.

## 2. Canonical Chapter 5 Discussion Architecture
1. **Summary of Findings**: Brief restatement of core empirical conclusions without repeating numerical tables.
2. **Contextualization with Prior Literature**:
   - Compare and contrast current findings with domestic and international empirical literature (2021–2026).
   - Address both consistent findings and discordant/contradictory literature.
3. **Theoretical Explanations**: Deep cognitive, behavioral, psychological, or organizational mechanisms explaining *why* the relationships emerged.
4. **Practical & Managerial Implications**: Actionable recommendations for clinicians, educators, policymakers, or industry professionals.
5. **Methodological Limitations**: Honest reflection on sample constraints, self-report biases, cross-sectional design, or generalizability.
6. **Future Research Directions**: Concrete proposals for longitudinal, experimental, or mixed-methods follow-up studies.

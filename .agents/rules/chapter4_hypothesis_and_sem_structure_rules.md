# CHAPTER 4 HYPOTHESIS & SEM STRUCTURE RULES
# Golden Rules for Dissertation Chapter 4 Architecture & Academic Writing Standards

This document codifies the mandatory architectural, structural, and narrative standards for Chapter 4 (یافته‌های پژوهش / Research Findings) across graduate theses and doctoral dissertations.

---

## 1. The 3-Table Standard for Relationship Hypotheses
For every hypothesis testing a relationship, linear prediction, or regression between predictors and a criterion:
- **Table 1: Bivariate Correlation Matrix**: Zero-order Pearson correlation coefficients ($r$), significance level ($p$), sample size ($N$), and descriptive statistics ($M, SD$).
- **Table 2: Model Summary & ANOVA**: Multiple correlation coefficient ($R$), coefficient of determination ($R^2$), adjusted $R^2$ ($\text{Adj } R^2$), standard error of the estimate ($SE$), and regression ANOVA test statistics ($F$, $df_1$, $df_2$, $p$).
- **Table 3: Regression Coefficients & Collinearity Diagnostics**: Unstandardized coefficients ($B, SE$), standardized beta ($\beta$), $t$-statistic, $p$-value, and collinearity metrics (Tolerance, VIF).
- **Narrative Placement**: A substantive 4-element academic narrative must be placed **directly above** each table caption.

---

## 2. Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) Macro-to-Micro Reporting Architecture
When a study investigates mediation, serial mediation, or structural paths deriving from an overarching Structural Equation Model (SEM) or Path Analysis:
1. **Macro Model Reporting First**: Before reporting individual mediation hypotheses (e.g. Hypotheses 3 to 8), the entire structural model must be reported macro-level first.
2. **Mandatory Macro SEM Elements**:
   - **Table A: Model Goodness-of-Fit Table**: Comparing initial vs. final/harnessed model across 11 indices ($\chi^2$, $df$, $\chi^2/df$, $p$, CFI, TLI, GFI, AGFI, NFI, RMSEA with 90% CI, SRMR) against Kline (2016) and Hu & Bentler (1999) cutoffs.
   - **Figure B: Structural Path Diagram**: High-resolution 300-DPI visual model with standardized path coefficients and $R^2$ values.
   - **Table C: Direct Structural Paths Table**: Complete parameter estimates ($B, SE, \beta, t/z, p$) for all direct paths.
   - **Table D: Indirect & Serial Mediation Paths Table**: 5,000 BCa bootstrap resamples reporting point estimates ($\beta$), bootstrap $SE$, 95% confidence intervals [LLCI, ULCI], exact $p$-values, and empirical verdicts.
3. **Dedicated Subsections for SEM-Related Hypotheses**:
   - Following the macro SEM section, each SEM-related hypothesis must have its own dedicated subsection.
   - Each subsection must feature an in-depth empirical interpretation linking the specific pathway from the macro tables, discussing effect sizes, confidence interval non-zero inclusion, relative mediator strength, and formal confirmation/rejection verdict.

---

## 3. Comprehensive Chapter 4 Summary (1 to 2 Full Pages)
- Conclude Chapter 4 with an extensive, multi-page synthesis spanning **1 to 2 full pages** (strictly prohibiting short single-paragraph summaries).
- Must systematically synthesize:
  1. Participant sample demographic profile synthesis.
  2. Main variable descriptive statistics and distributional normality.
  3. Parametric assumption verification and Harman single-factor common method variance diagnostics.
  4. Structural model fit evaluation and data harnessing achievements (e.g. RMSEA optimization).
  5. Direct and indirect structural mediation results synthesis.
  6. **Master Hypotheses Decision Matrix Table (`جدول ماتریس جمع‌بندی نهایی فرضیات`)**: Hypothesis number, statement, statistical test, key metrics, $p$-value, and final verdict.
  7. **Conceptual Transition Bridge to Chapter 5 (`پل مفهومی و انتقال به فصل پنجم`)**: Preparing the empirical findings for theoretical and clinical discussion in Chapter 5 without introducing literature comparisons in Chapter 4.

---

## 4. The Gold Standard for Academic Table Explanations (Doctoral Caliber)
Table explanations must adhere to the highest academic standards of scholarship. **Superficial, 1-2 sentence, tiny, or juvenile explanations are strictly forbidden.**

### 4.1. Demographic Tables
- Concise, clear reporting of frequencies and percentage distributions across categories is sufficient.

### 4.2. Substantive & Inferential Tables (Mandatory Deep Scholarly Analysis)
All inferential, descriptive, correlational, and structural tables must have extensive, multi-paragraph scholarly analyses:

1. **Bivariate Correlation Matrix (`ماتریس همبستگی پیرسون`)**:
   - **NEVER omit or gloss over the correlation matrix.**
   - Must provide an extensive, multi-paragraph scholarly analysis detailing:
     - The sign, magnitude ($|r|$), and statistical significance of every bivariate pairing.
     - Confirmation of construct distinctiveness and discriminant validity.
     - Verification of the absence of extreme multicollinearity ($r < .85$, Kline, 2016).
     - The alignment of observed zero-order relationships with theoretical expectations.

2. **SEM Goodness-of-Fit Table**:
   - Provide comprehensive, doctoral-level academic narrative detailing:
     - The mathematical interpretation of the chi-square discrepancy function and sensitivity to sample size.
     - Individual evaluation of each absolute (SRMR, RMSEA), incremental/comparative (CFI, TLI, NFI), and parsimony-adjusted (AGFI) index.
     - Methodological justification for data harnessing (pruning multivariate high-residual outliers to achieve close fit $\text{RMSEA} \le .050$ while maintaining sample retention $\ge 75\%$).
     - Formal confirmation of structural validity per Kline (2016) and Hu & Bentler (1999).

3. **SEM Direct Paths Table**:
   - Detailed parameter-by-parameter analysis:
     - Unstandardized weights ($B$), standard errors ($SE$), standardized loadings ($\beta$), critical ratios ($z/t$), and exact $p$-values.
     - Relative ranking of predictor impacts on endogenous mediators and outcomes.

4. **SEM Indirect & Serial Mediation Bootstrap Table**:
   - Exhaustive breakdown of all simple and serial indirect pathways:
     - 5,000 bootstrap resamples with Bias-Corrected and Accelerated (BCa) 95% confidence intervals.
     - The mathematical criterion of non-zero interval inclusion/exclusion ($LLCI > 0$ or $ULCI < 0$).
     - Specific discussion of suppression effects, full vs. partial mediation, and cognitive absorption dynamics.

5. **Hypothesis Deep Dives**:
   - Multi-paragraph, rigorous empirical dissection for each hypothesis.
   - Contextualize the statistical magnitude, practical significance, clinical implications, and definitive empirical verdict.

6. **Strict Empirical Boundary**:
   - Chapter 4 narrative remains strictly grounded in **empirical numbers and statistical mechanics**.
   - **Zero external literature citations** (e.g. Beck, Bandura, Gross, Hayes) and **zero speculative psychological interpretations** in Chapter 4; all theoretical comparisons belong exclusively in **Chapter 5**.

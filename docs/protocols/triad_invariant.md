# Triad Artifact Invariant Protocol (Directive 3)

## Purpose
Monolithic drafting prompts ("Draft Chapter 4 in full") are strictly prohibited. Every analytical micro-stage and individual hypothesis must produce a synchronized triad of artifacts on disk before chapter assembly.

## The Triad Elements
For every stage, the following three synchronized files must physically exist on disk:
1. **Structured Data / Statistics (`.json`)**:
   - Exact numerical parameters, test statistics ($t, F, \beta, p$), degrees of freedom, and sample sizes.
2. **Human-Readable Markdown Narrative & APA Tables (`.md`)**:
   - Narrative placing findings above tables, APA 7 3-line tables, and Persian leading zero compliance (`۰.۰۰۱ > p`).
3. **OpenXML Institutional Word Document (`.docx`)**:
   - Institutional typography (`B Nazanin` 13pt body, `B Titr` 12-18pt headings, `Times New Roman` Latin), decoupled LTR numbers ($-0.32$), and native OMML math equations.
